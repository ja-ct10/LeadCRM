# User Journey: The Guest (Potential Client / Lead)

In a CRM system, a "Guest" is essentially a potential client or external lead interacting with the public-facing side of the company. Here is the step-by-step journey of how a guest experiences the system:

### 1. Discovery & Inquiry (The First Contact)
* **Action:** The guest visits the Camxian Technologies website or landing page and decides to inquire about a service or product.
* **Process:** They fill out a digital contact form. Right before clicking submit, they are prompted to read and check a simple Data Privacy consent box to ensure legal compliance.
* **System Automation:** The system instantly captures their information, checks automatically to ensure they aren't a duplicate entry, and drops their profile securely into the CRM's Sales Pipeline as a "New Lead."

### 2. Automated Welcome (The Quick Follow-Up)
* **Action:** The guest completes their submission and goes about their day.
* **System Automation:** Behind the scenes, the CRM instantly triggers a personalized Welcome Email and an SMS text message to the guest's correctly formatted Philippine phone number (+63). The SMS automatically includes the legally required telecom disclaimers safely at the bottom.

### 3. Engagement (The Human Connection)
* **Action:** The guest receives a tailored email or direct phone call from a Camxian Sales or Support Representative.
* **Process:** They discuss the project, ask questions, and negotiate the terms. As they talk, the internal team moves the guest through the CRM's deal pipelines (e.g., from "Contacted" to "Proposal Sent").

### 4. Secure Payment (The Transaction)
* **Action:** The guest officially agrees to the proposal and is ready to pay.
* **Process:** The sales team triggers a custom payment request from the CRM. The guest receives a secure, direct payment link in their email or via SMS. 
* **System Automation:** The guest clicks the link and safely completes the transaction using the integrated PayMongo gateway. The CRM automatically updates their lead status to "Closed/Won" the moment the payment is verified.

### 5. Data Privacy Rights (The Aftercare)
* **Action:** Fast forward to the future—the guest decides they want to see what information the company holds, or they want their information completely erased.
* **Process:** The guest contacts Camxian Technologies to execute their data rights. 
* **System Automation:** Thanks to strict compliance features, the system easily exports all of the guest's personal data into an easy-to-read machine format for them to review, or allows the admin to permanently delete their records in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.
